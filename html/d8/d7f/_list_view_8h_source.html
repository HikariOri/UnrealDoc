31/*
32 * A virtualized list that allows up to thousands of items to be displayed.
33 *
34 * An important distinction to keep in mind here is "Item" vs. "Entry"
35 * The list itself is based on a list of n items, but only creates as many entry widgets as can fit on screen.
36 * For example, a scrolling ListView of 200 items with 5 currently visible will only have created 5 entry widgets.
37 *
38 * To make a widget usable as an entry in a ListView, it must inherit from the IUserObjectListEntry interface.
39 */
