◆ PivotType

EMeshMergePivotType FMeshMergingSettings::PivotType

Determines how the pivot point of the merged mesh is calculated.

  • WorldOrigin: Pivot at (0,0,0)
  • Automatic: Pivot at the first merged component's location (default)
  • Custom: Pivot at CustomPivotLocation
