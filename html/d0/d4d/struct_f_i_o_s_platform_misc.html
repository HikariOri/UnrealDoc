enum class FIOSPlatformMisc::EIOSAuthMicrophonePermissionStatus : uint8
strong

Tri-state microphone (Apple "record") permission query, parallel to GetNotificationAuthorizationStatus. HasRecordPermission can't distinguish "user explicitly denied" from "user has never been asked", which callers need in order to decide between triggering the OS popup and opening app settings.
