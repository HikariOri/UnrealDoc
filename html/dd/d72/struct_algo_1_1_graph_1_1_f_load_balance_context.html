◆ OutRootAssignments

TArray<TArray<FVertexFVertex> >* Algo::Graph::FLoadBalanceContext::OutRootAssignments = nullptr

Optional. If non-null, will be assigned NumBuckets elements. The nth element corresponds to the nth element of OutAssignments, and records the roots of the graph that are present in that bucket. In the absence of cycles, roots are all vertices with no referencers, also known as no incoming edges. The list of vertices reachable from the roots spans the graph. In the presence of cycles at the root level, an arbitrary vertex from each rooted cycle will be chosen as the root for that cycle.

Each root will appear in exactly one bucket. All vertices in the bucket will be reachable from one of the roots in the bucket.
