详细描述

Data parsed from the header in the package. Might be stored either as a ZenPackageHeader or LinkerLoad PackageHeader, depending on the EPackageHeaderFormat of the package. Provides an interface for the data needed to interpret the bytes of the exports of the package, such as the NameMap.
