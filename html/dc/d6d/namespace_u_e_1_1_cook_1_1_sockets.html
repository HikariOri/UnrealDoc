◆ CreateListenSocket()

FSocket * UE::Cook::Sockets::CreateListenSocket ( int32 & InOutPort,
TSharedPtr< FInternetAddr > & OutAddr,
FString & OutConnectAuthority,
const TCHAR * SocketDebugName,
FString & OutErrorReason )

Create a socket to listen for incoming connections on the given port.

参数
InOutPortOn input, the desired listening port. On output, the selected port if desired listening port was 0, otherwise unchanged.
OutAddrThe address being listened to at the OS socket level, e.g. 0.0.0.0:<port>.
OutConnectAuthorityThe URI authority other processes should connect to, e.g. 192.168.1.10:<port>.
SocketDebugNameString used in logs sent for the socket.
OutErrorReasonReason why creation failed if return value is nullptr.
返回
Valid socket or nullptr on failure.
