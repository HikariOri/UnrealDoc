◆ RadiusOrComponentScale

FVector4f FBounds4::RadiusOrComponentScale

Sphere radii for the bounding sphere of 4 texture/mesh static instances or component scale for dynamic instances. When the bounds row is owned by FSimpleStreamableAssetManager (SSAM), this field always carries StreamingScaleFactor (i.e., the ComponentScale interpretation) regardless of whether the instance is static or dynamic; the legacy "sphere radius for static" interpretation does not apply on SSAM-owned rows. Consumers that branch on static vs dynamic must account for the SSAM ownership case.
