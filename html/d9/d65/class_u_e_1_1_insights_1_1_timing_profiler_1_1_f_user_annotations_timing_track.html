详细描述

Renders user annotations as colored vertical markers on the Insights timeline. Follows the FMarkersTimingTrack pattern: cached draw state and change-number dirty checking. Placement: TopDocked or BottomDocked, order = FTimingTrackOrder::Markers + 1 (below system Bookmarks).
