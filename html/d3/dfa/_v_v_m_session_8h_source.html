18
19Why --> References to devices, widgets, characters, entities, components (literally anything scene graph)
20etc are all likely to become 'invalid' in a sense between rounds when a world is reloaded (BPVM / UE).
21Thus, we can't reference them safely from weak_maps unless we make them _all_ invalidatable/disposable
