详细描述

Drag & drop handler for hierarchy categories in the details panel. Supports both dragging (source) and dropping (target). Uses FHierarchyDragDropOp for dragging (categories are not properties), and accepts both FHierarchyDragDropOp and FPropertyBagDetailsDragDropOp for drops (properties dragged onto categories use the latter).
