template<typename T>
struct TListTypeTraits< T *, typename TEnableIf< TPointerIsConvertibleFromTo< T, UObjectBase >::Value >::Type >

Lists of pointer types only work if the pointers are deriving from UObject*. In addition to testing and setting the pointers to nullptr, Lists of UObjects will serialize the objects they are holding onto.
