A torus is the solid formed by revolving a circular disc about a specified coplanar axis. MajorRadius is the distance from the axis to the center of the defining disc, and MinorRadius is the radius of the defining disc, where MajorRadius > MinorRadius > 0.0.

The torus computed at the origin with Z axis. It is placed at its final position and orientation by the Matrix

The bounds of the cone are defined as follow: Bounds[EIso::IsoU].Min = MajorStartAngle Bounds[EIso::IsoU].Max = MajorEndAngle Bounds[EIso::IsoV].Min = MinorStartAngle Bounds[EIso::IsoV].Max = MinorEndAngle
